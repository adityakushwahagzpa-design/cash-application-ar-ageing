# Cash Application & AR Ageing Tracker | Excel

An Excel cash application and AR ageing tracker that matches **9,362 customer receipts to 10,000 invoices (₹131.9 crore)** for a sample industrial distributor, FY2024-25.

## Build
- Receipts matched by remittance reference and flagged as full, short or part payments, or unapplied cash
- Open balances aged into Not Due, 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ day buckets (INDEX-MATCH, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, IF)

## Key findings (31 March 2025)
- **₹24.9 crore** still open, 21% of it more than 90 days past due
- **₹74.2 lakh** of short-payment deductions and **₹97.7 lakh** of unapplied cash to resolve
